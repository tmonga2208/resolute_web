# The Resolute Mind

_The Resolute Mind_ is a website for psychological services, created for **Kriti Monga**, a professional sports psychologist.

The platform is designed to showcase her expertise, provide resources related to mental wellness in sports, and allow athletes and clients to learn more about the importance of mental strength in performance.

## 🧠 Purpose

To support athletes and individuals in achieving mental clarity, resilience, and focus through professional psychological services.

## 👩‍⚕️ About Kriti Monga

Kriti Monga is a certified **sports psychologist** with experience in helping athletes improve their mental well-being, performance, and focus. This website serves as a professional platform for her services and content.
